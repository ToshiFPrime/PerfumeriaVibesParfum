# Taste-Driven Luxury E-Commerce Standard

This skill codifies the principles of "Taste" in modern interface craftsmanship: restraint, editorial typography, atmospheric depth, sensory luxury, and emotional resonance.

## Core Directives for Vibes Parfum

### 1. The Luxury Dark Palette (Negro, Plomo, Blanco)
- **Base Canvas:** Deepest obsidian onyx (`#09090b` / `#0c0c0e`), creating high dramatic contrast for perfume glass and brand photography.
- **Plomo & Slates (Graphite Layers):**
  - Surface layer 1: `#121215` (Cards, drawers, modals)
  - Surface layer 2: `#18181c` (Nested cards, variant pills, filters)
  - Plomo borders: `#27272a` (Subtle container demarcation)
  - Metallic chrome touches: Linear gradients transitioning from white to silver-zinc (`#d4d4d8` to `#71717a`).
- **Whites:** High-purity white (`#ffffff` and `#f4f4f5`) for brand typography, headings, and CTA contrast.
- **WhatsApp Direct Accent:** Tastefully integrated emerald green (`#25D366`) restricted strictly to customer contact and WhatsApp order actions.

### 2. Olfactory Sensory Presentation
- **The Fragrance Experience is Invisible:** Because users cannot smell through a screen, visual design must evoke temperature, texture, weight, and emotion.
- **The Olfactory Pyramid:** Represent top notes (ligeras y volátiles), heart notes (el alma del perfume), and base notes (fijación profunda en la piel) with visual weight, clear iconography, and layered hierarchy.
- **Decant vs. Full Bottle Storytelling:** Highlight that decants allow exploration without commitment, while full presentation bottles are statements of personal identity.

### 3. Editorial Layout & Hierarchy
- Use generous negative space around high-end bottles.
- Combine modern sans-serif typography with editorial serif accents for luxury prestige.
- Filter panels should feel like a sommelier's scent library, with interactive tags, pill selectors, and immediate sensory feedback.
- Avoid cluttered badge bombardment. Use minimal, refined pill badges (e.g., "EDP", "Extrait", "10ml", "Nicho").
